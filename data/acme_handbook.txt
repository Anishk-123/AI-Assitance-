ACME TECH EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees get a one-hour lunch break.

2. Leave Policy
Every employee receives 24 paid annual leave days per year and 10 sick leave days per year. A maximum of 8 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 10 days.

3. Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to approval from their reporting manager.

4. Probation and Notice Period
The probation period for new employees is 3 months. After confirmation, the notice period for resignation is 60 days.

5. Benefits
Health insurance covers the employee, spouse and up to 2 children with a coverage of 5 lakh INR. Each employee gets a learning budget of 30,000 INR per year for courses and certifications. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

6. Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate receive a referral bonus of 25,000 INR once the referred employee completes 6 months in the company.

7. Expense Reimbursement
Expenses must be submitted with receipts and are reimbursed within 30 days of submission.

8. HR Contact
For any questions, contact HR at hr@acmetech.example.
